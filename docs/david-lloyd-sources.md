# David Lloyd demo sources

Verified 29 September 2026. This is an independent illustrative Petey demo. Trainer names, portraits, qualifications, coaching biographies, dashboard activity and operational controls are fictional sample data, not actual David Lloyd staff, availability, enquiries or bookings.

## Scope and club data

The official [London city listing](https://www.davidlloyd.co.uk/gyms-in-your-city/) states 20 London/Greater London clubs. The demo uses those 20 clubs within Greater London; historic postal counties do not change the geographical scope. Chelsea Harbour Club is separately branded and excluded. Home/access eligibility retains all 20 clubs even though the fictional profile sample covers Raynes Park (4), Kingston (3) and Colliers Wood (3). Other clubs return an honest empty sample.

Addresses come from each official club page's PostalAddress data. Coordinates come from that page's linked Google Maps location, not a postcode centroid. For Acton Park and Kensington, the short map links were resolved to their place coordinates rather than the map viewport centre. All records use verifiedAt `2026-09-29`.

## Personal training and pricing

[David Lloyd personal training](https://www.davidlloyd.co.uk/personal-training/) describes personalised goal-based coaching and fitness assessment. [Gym FAQs](https://www.davidlloyd.co.uk/gym/) describe app booking and subscription savings, without establishing the individual prices or diaries of this demo's fictional trainers. [Membership options](https://www.davidlloyd.co.uk/membership-options/) makes package and club choice relevant to membership pricing, with personal training an additional service.

No current universal PT price floor, hourly rate, session duration or trainer-tier pricing is verified. The demo therefore asks for an arbitrary **per-session** budget or accepts “Not sure yet”/“Flexible”. Budget is retained as an unconfirmed preference and never filters or ranks trainers. Third Space's rates and Personal/Elite tiers do not carry over. A historical [official PT blog](https://blog.davidlloyd.co.uk/david-lloyd-clubs-news/the-benefits-of-working-out-with-a-personal-trainer-at-david-lloyd-clubs/) mentions 45-minute sessions; this is not used as a current universal session length. No official current “PT 55” assumption is used.

[Raynes Park](https://www.davidlloyd.co.uk/clubs/raynes-park/) advertises racquet facilities, indoor/outdoor swimming and personal training. Daniel's fictional profile therefore supports strength and conditioning for tennis/padel. It does not offer lessons. [Kingston](https://www.davidlloyd.co.uk/clubs/kingston/gym/) and [Colliers Wood](https://www.davidlloyd.co.uk/clubs/colliers-wood/gym/) advertise personal training including older-adult, mobility and beginner needs. Sophie's fictional profile emphasises older-adult strength, balance and mobility. Colliers Wood's [main page](https://www.davidlloyd.co.uk/clubs/colliers-wood/) says it is open. Neither Kingston nor Colliers Wood is represented as a racquets club. Tennis, padel and swimming lessons are separate services, outside this PT matching demo.

## Membership and access

The [membership page](https://www.davidlloyd.co.uk/memberships/), [Help Centre](https://www.davidlloyd.co.uk/help-centre/) and linked [club-tier guide](https://res.cloudinary.com/softwire-david-lloyd/image/upload/v1751556632/Terms%20and%20conditions/club-tiers-december-24_lbxtnl.pdf) indicate that membership benefits and other-club access vary; the app is the up-to-date account-specific source. The guide itself is dated December 2024 and says access can change. The currently linked [membership terms](https://res.cloudinary.com/softwire-david-lloyd/image/upload/v1656676416/Terms%20and%20conditions/terms-and-conditions.pdf) include home-club usage and access conditions. These documents do not verify a demo visitor's individual entitlements.

The demo records a volunteered package name as free text, without requiring it or translating Platinum, Diamond, Club Plus, Club or legacy names into an access tier. Member eligibility is **confirmed home clubs plus explicitly confirmed additional current access, minus exclusions/waitlists**. “Only these clubs” refinements remove previously named clubs outside that list. A known home club is enough to continue; non-members provide one training area. The demo never claims to have checked a membership account.

## Visual provenance

The [official homepage](https://www.davidlloyd.co.uk/) was inspected on the verification date. Its light brand palette informed cream `#FCFCF6`, plum `#82285F` and text `#474A4A`. The first David Lloyd CLUBS inline script-logo SVG (`viewBox="0 0 199 78"`) was extracted; its path geometry is retained and fills changed to plum. The local copies are `david-lloyd-demo/public/david-lloyd-logo.svg` and `src/david-lloyd-demo-admin/david-lloyd-logo.svg`. Existing DM Sans remains the UI font by design; the demo does not redistribute the official site's proprietary font.

The ten synthetic identities and monochrome portraits are reused unchanged from the approved Third Space fictional sample. The David Lloyd files have isolated `dl-demo-` IDs and asset paths. Emma remains the hero portrait. Only fictional club assignments and relevant profile details change. No public trainer photography or biographies are imported.

## Deployment configuration

The existing project organisation policy rejects public `allUsers` IAM grants. These callables follow the verified Third Space transport pattern: deploy source with `invoker: "private"`, then run the two service-specific `gcloud run services update ... --no-invoker-iam-check` commands in the [functions deployment instructions](../functions-david-lloyd/README.md). This sets `run.googleapis.com/invoker-iam-disabled: 'true'` only on `rundavidlloydonboardingturnv1` and `matchdavidlloydtrainersv1`. App Check remains mandatory; anonymous transport does not bypass callable attestation, transcript validation or rate limits. No wider IAM permission changes are required.
