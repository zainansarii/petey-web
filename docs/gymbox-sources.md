## Directory and membership

The [official PT directory](https://gymbox.com/personal-trainers/) exposes ten club filters: Bank, Ealing, Elephant & Castle, Farringdon, Finsbury Park, Holborn, Old Street, Victoria, Westfield Shepherds Bush, Westfield Stratford. Some directory cards repeat: no real trainer count is inferred. Accept “Elephant and Castle”; “Westfield London” is the official URL/footer alias for Westfield Shepherds Bush.

The [membership page](https://gymbox.com/memberships/) distinguishes home-club single-gym access from all-ten-club access, separately from monthly, three-month and twelve-month commitment. Student/corporate labels, prices, term and promotional PT offers cannot establish access. Do not copy membership prices into per-session PT rates.

[Terms, chapter 15](https://gymbox.com/terms-and-conditions/) (page dated 29 June 2026) say PTs are self-employed freelancers and the session agreement is with the PT; personal training is restricted to members aged 18+. Only Gymbox-approved trainers/instructors may provide training. Non-member demo exploration is therefore not a claim of booking eligibility; guest passes/Wellhub are not verified PT eligibility.

The [booking FAQ](https://www.support.gymbox.com/support/solutions/articles/103000386102-how-do-i-book-a-personal-training-session-) confirms PT costs extra and may be arranged through TEAMUP or directly with a trainer. No universal rate or session duration is stated. Demo rate remains null / “Rates on enquiry”.

The [personal-training page](https://gymbox.com/personal-training/) offers in-gym and online services; its form states that a freelance trainer will contact the enquirer. The synthetic demo covers in-club matching only. Group classes and Out the Box are separate services.

## Club-specific facility conflict

Bank's [combat change notice](https://www.support.gymbox.com/support/solutions/articles/103000406050-why-are-the-current-combat-classes-coming-to-an-end-) says the boxing ring is removed for a larger functional/hybrid area and Boxing/Muay Thai leave the timetable from 1 July 2026; combat conditioning is planned when the new area reopens. Its [equipment notice](https://www.support.gymbox.com/support/solutions/articles/103000406052-will-combat-equipment-will-still-be-available-) confirms boxing bags remain.

The current Bank page still has older FAQ text mentioning an Olympic boxing ring and boxing classes, while its main content describes the old boxing room converted to hybrid space. Prefer the dated specific change notice: no Bank ring/cage claim; no inference that all boxing-related personal coaching is unavailable. Farringdon's current page separately lists a combat area, Olympic lifting, free weights, functional/hybrid and HYROX-affiliated training. Do not project facilities between clubs.
